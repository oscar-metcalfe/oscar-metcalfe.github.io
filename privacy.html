Metcalfe-hub privacy

Metcalfe-hub is a personal dashboard used by one household. It is not offered to the public.

What it reads: with read-only access to Google Tasks, it reads the owner's own task lists to show the next task that is due.

What it keeps: only the title and due date of that next task, on the owner's own device. It does not change, share or sell any data, and it does not read any other Google data.

How to stop it: remove "Metcalfe-hub" at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
